Methods and literature

This page maps CausalMediation APIs to the papers that define the estimands, identification conditions, and estimators implemented in Julia. Closest R companions include crumble and medoutcon. Full bibliographic entries are in References. Keys such as diaz2020mediation match references.bib in the CDCS book. Engine naming (:interventional, not "RI") is summarised in Naming.

Counterfactual data and mediation

Counterfactual-data designs can provide information beyond ordinary observational and interventional distributions for some nested or path-specific queries. That possibility is relevant to natural direct effects, but identification of the counterfactual query belongs upstream in CausalDynamics. This package currently estimates the mediation functionals supported by its MediationSpec and does not claim a general counterfactual-data identification algorithm. A future bridge should consume an explicit identification certificate rather than infer cross-world information from the mediator data frame.

Interventional (randomised intermediate) effects

Scientific problem. Natural direct and indirect effects require cross-world independence assumptions that fail when a post-treatment variable confounds the mediator–outcome relation (intermediate confounding). Interventional (randomised interventional) effects replace the natural mediator law with a random draw from an interventional mediator distribution, recovering TE / NDE / NIE decompositions under weaker conditions (Vansteelandt & Daniel, 2017; Díaz & Hejazi, 2020; Liu et al., 2024).

Continuous MTP. Treatment shifts follow CausalTargeted ShiftPolicy (z-scale or natural scale, quantile clamps). Nested Monte Carlo draws mediators under shifted treatment; outcome regressions are cross-fitted Super Learners.

Factor treatment. Interventional TE / NDE / NIE also admit a recode MTP on a finite A (DiscreteTreatmentPolicy on both MediationSpec arms; default identity versus recode). Outcome and mediator regressions dummy-code A; treatment density ratios reuse CausalTargeted Díaz–Williams classifiers. Nested Monte Carlo for continuous M is unchanged. Natural / organic / controlled-direct families, nonempty moc, and run_mediation_grid remain numeric-A only. Mixed ShiftPolicy / discrete arms throw. The reserved RecantingTwinMediation compatibility type always errors; it has no estimator.

Uncertainty contract. Constant discrete treatment arms, empty moc, uniform weights and estimator=:onestep use both outcome and mediator-stage residual corrections. The mediator correction is part of the two-stage EIF; an identity density ratio does not justify dropping it. Check overlap and nuisance-model assumptions before interpreting the resulting intervals. Static numeric-binary and factor-arm routes preserve exact zero propensity weights off the assigned arm; numeric-binary positive weights retain their existing truncation. Remaining finite-sample undercoverage in the synthetic calibration is documented in the CDCS uncertainty report; these intervals are asymptotic and have not demonstrated nominal coverage throughout the tested small-sample settings.

weight_truncation::Bool=true is the compatibility default capped route. For otherwise eligible static-arm one-step inference, point estimates are preserved while inferential SEs, confidence limits and influence curves are unavailable with inference_status = :truncated_weights, even if no observed weight reaches the cap. This status follows the configured route; it is not an empirical cap-activation test. Prior unsupported or trivial statuses take precedence. Set weight_truncation=false only for genuinely static binary or factor arms to request the untruncated EIF. Numerical and Super Learner guards still apply; opting out of weight truncation does not remove the usual positivity/overlap, nuisance-model or moment assumptions and does not guarantee finite-sample coverage. Non-static or continuous policies reject this opt-out. In particular, continuous ShiftPolicy grids and row-dependent identity-versus-recode policies are not static-arm targets.

Other routes retain point estimates but return NaN inferential SEs and limits, with an inference_status explaining the restriction. These include plug-in fits, the current outcome-only :tmle fluctuation, factual-treatment dependent recodes, continuous clamped shifts, intermediate-confounder targets and IPCW fits without a matching joint score. Continuous clamping creates boundary masses. Nonempty moc requires the additional stages of its nested functional, rather than the empty-moc EIF. No estimator or target is silently substituted to supply an interval. Identical arms give an exact zero contrast. mediation_n_mc_sweep diagnoses integration error; it does not measure sampling uncertainty. The existing antithetic draws are retained.

Minimal DAG (no intermediate confounder):

using CausalDynamics, Graphs

g = DiGraph(4)
add_edge!(g, 1, 2); add_edge!(g, 1, 3); add_edge!(g, 1, 4)
add_edge!(g, 2, 3); add_edge!(g, 2, 4); add_edge!(g, 3, 4)
id = identify(
    g, MediationQuery(:A, :Y, [:M]; effect_kind = :interventional);
    node_names = Dict(1 => :W, 2 => :A, 3 => :M, 4 => :Y),
)
(id.strategy, id.adjustment, id.mediators, id.moc)
(:mediation_interventional, [:W], [:M], Symbol[])

With intermediate confounding, CausalDynamics can populate id.moc; estimation must pass those symbols into MediationSpec.

Natural, organic, and controlled direct effects

TopicPrimary sourcesCausalMediation surface
Natural direct/indirectPearl (2001); Robins & Greenland (1992); VanderWeele (2015)NaturalMediation (empty moc only)
Organic effectsLok (2015)OrganicMediation
Controlled direct effectVanderWeele (2015)ControlledDirect(:M => …)

Natural effects share the identification gate with CausalDynamics: nonempty moc throws. Organic and controlled-direct paths are available for specialised contrasts; interpret them against the cited definitions, not as drop-in replacements for interventional TE/NDE/NIE.

ControlledDirect holds a continuous mediator at the specified value and averages cross-fitted outcome-regression contrasts. Omitting estimator selects :plugin for this effect; explicit :onestep and :tmle throw. A fixed value of a continuous mediator has no ordinary nonparametric influence function, so the treatment-only residual correction is invalid. The returned point estimate depends on the outcome model. Standard errors and confidence limits are NaN until a suitable inference method is implemented. Nonempty moc is refused: its post-treatment distribution would also need to be integrated under each treatment arm.

Nested units versus clustered mediation

When observations nest in clusters, CausalDynamics owns generative nested $U$ (RandomEffectSpec, unroll_hierarchical_dag). CausalTargeted owns sampling hierarchy via cluster= on repeated-outcome / parametric MSM (cluster-robust IF sandwich). Sampling dependence and causal interference are distinct: changing an ordinary mediation estimator's covariance does not identify a joint mediator intervention. Hierarchical longitudinal mediation ($A_t$, $M_t$) remains deferred (BOUNDARIES).

Clustered mediation

This package provides a Julia-native implementation of the static clustered interventional mediation reference of Ham, Cheng & Li (2026, arXiv:2611.11903). The public surface is ClusterMediationSpec / run_cluster_mediation.

TopicPrimary sourcesCausalMediation surface
Cluster-randomised mediation with post-treatment confoundingHam, Cheng & Li (2026), arXiv:2611.11903ClusterMediationSpec, run_cluster_mediation

The estimator targets joint-mediator stochastic interventions with intermediate confounding and within-cluster interference under a restricted Gaussian backend and cluster-level cross-fitting. It is a different estimand from the MediationSpec nested-MC path, does not extend that path's uncertainty contract, and does not consume an automatic MediationQuery certificate (identification=:user_assumed).

For the same clustered targets, fit_bayesian_cluster_mediation and bayesian_mediation_effects provide a separate experimental parametric hierarchical Bayesian route (reusing ClusterMediationSpec with structured variational inference and optional correction = :psis), trading density-ratio instability for explicit Gaussian random-intercept mechanisms and priors. Full targets, equations, diagnostics, and limits: Clustered mediation.

Recanting twins and path-specific effects

Recanting-twin constructions can define path-specific effects in settings where natural effects are not identified (Vo–Díaz and related work). The current RecantingTwinMediation type is retained as a reserved compatibility name, but every estimation entry point throws an explanatory ArgumentError. Its former implementation only relabelled an interventional decomposition and therefore did not estimate the requested path-specific target. When an interventional estimand is intended, request InterventionalMediation() explicitly; its own identification and inference conditions still apply. The simulate_recanting_twin_mediation helper remains available to generate synthetic data for graph and model demonstrations, not to imply that the path-specific estimator is implemented.

Cross-fitting, Super Learner, and diagnostics

Nuisances reuse CausalTargeted profiles (DEFAULT_SL_LEARNERS, SMALL_N_SL_LEARNERS, …). Fold caches (MediationFoldCache) fit a single CausalTargeted covariate schema on the cleaned analysis frame so string and categorical columns encode consistently across folds.

Missing outcomes and covariates

Mediation runners forward incompleteness to CausalTargeted handle_missing_data. run_mediation_grid, run_mediation_scalar, and conjugate_mediation_bootstrap (via run_mediation_scalar_ppl) accept handle_missing (:drop default; :ipcw reweights supported interventional, natural, and organic influence summaries and the controlled direct plug-in contrast; for the conjugate bootstrap it draws rows with IPCW probabilities; :impute / :ipcw_impute for covariate gaps). Missing mediators are treated as covariates for that policy (complete-case or mean-impute with indicators), not as a separate mediation-specific missingness estimand. Structural MAR/MNAR claims remain CausalDynamics certificates; this package does not invent fills inside the EIF. Full policy catalogue: CausalTargeted Missingness.

run_tmle3_nde is retained as a fail-fast migration entry point and throws without fitting. Numeric static-arm users should migrate to run_mediation_scalar(...; estimator = :onestep, weight_truncation = false); static discrete-arm users should use run_mediation with static policies. The function does not silently switch to the package's one-step or targeted route.

Do not pass learners=(:mean,) alone for treatment contrasts.

Use validate_mediation_sweep before archiving a nested-MC stability table. It checks the result-table contract (required columns, positive n_mc, finite estimates and non-negative standard errors); it does not test exchangeability, positivity or other causal assumptions, which remain part of the certificate.

Optional Lux Riesz representers load via weakdep (fit_riesz_representer after using Lux, fitting a single-hidden-layer Lux.Chain random-feature map with a closed-form ridge readout); riesz_available() reports whether the extension is loaded. Riesz nuisances are separate from representation (encoder → codes).

High-dimensional mediators via codes

Raw spectra or images are not MediationSpec mediators. Compress them first with CausalDynamics RepresentationSpec and encode_to_panel, then treat the code columns as mediators:

using CausalDynamics, CausalMediation, CausalTargeted, DataFrames

# S::Matrix (n × p), df has :A, :W, :Y
spec_rep = RepresentationSpec(
    :spectrum, [:z1, :z2], encode;  # encode: n×p → n×2 (Lux CNN/MLP or linear map)
    role = :definitional,           # or :measurement
)
cert = representation_certificate(spec_rep)
wide = encode_to_panel(df, S, spec_rep)

med = MediationSpec(:A, :Y; mediators = [:z1, :z2], covariates = [:W])
# Fit / freeze the encoder on training folds before cross-fitted EIF (DeepMed habit).
# res = run_mediation(med, wide; …)
  • :definitional: the estimand is about the codes themselves (M := g_θ(S)).
  • :measurement: codes are noisy observations of a latent biological mediator; state that in run metadata via cert.
  • Do not expand the Lux Riesz scaffold into a full CNN here; keep Riesz as an optional nuisance for high-dim moc / features already in tabular form.

Executable sketch: CausalDynamics examples/representation_bridge.jl. Application stress (codes → mediation / LMTP): CausalTargeted deepscmestimation_stress.qmd. Additive generative L3 on codes (abduction) lives in CausalDynamics Phase 2b, not in the mediation EIF path.

What we deliberately do not claim

  • Full option parity with R crumble / medoutcon / medRCT
  • Survival or competing-risks mediation (document as future scope)
  • Replacing CausalTargeted for non-mediated LMTP
